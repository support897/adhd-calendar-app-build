Pace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Pace is a personal day planner. This policy is short because the app collects almost nothing.

What Pace collects

If you use Pace without an account, nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, account or not. Pace works fully without an account; then everything stays on this device, as below.

If you make an account, your calendar saves to your account so it is there on your other devices. It is stored with Google Firebase under your own sign-in, and only you can open it — the app's rules let your account read your calendar and no one else's. Pace still works fully without an account; then everything stays on this device, as before. We never sell data, never show ads, and the person who runs Pace sees only counts (how many people joined), never your calendar. Delete your account in Settings and everything saved to it is deleted with it.

Where your information lives

Without an account, everything you put into Pace — your tasks, steps, notes, to-do items, habits, feelings check-ins and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it. We cannot see it, back it up or restore it. With an account, that same information also saves to your account (see above), so it is there on your other devices; your device keeps its own copy too.

Optional features

Handwriting: if you choose to scan a photo of handwriting, the reading tool is included inside Pace. Your photo is read on your device and is never uploaded by Pace.

Bringing a calendar in: if you choose to import a calendar, Pace reads the calendar file you pick — once, and only to copy your events in. The file is read on your device; Pace does not connect to your old calendar service, and it never changes or deletes anything in your old calendar. The events then live on your device like everything else in Pace.

Voice typing: if you tap Speak, your words are turned into text by your device's own speech service (on a phone, the one built into the phone; in a browser, the browser's). That service may process the audio itself to turn your words into text; Pace collects and stores nothing. Pace asks for the microphone only when you tap Speak, keeps no recording, and the text it makes lives on your device like everything else here. Typing always works exactly the same.

Voice: if you choose to dictate, Pace uses your device or keyboard's own speech service. What that service does with your voice is covered by your device maker's policy, not ours. Typing always works instead.

Reminders: Pace's reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Children

Pace is a general-audience productivity app. It is not designed for or directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with a new date at the top.
