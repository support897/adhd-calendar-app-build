Pace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Pace stores the information you choose to add to your day planner. You can use it without an account.

What Pace stores

Without an account, your tasks, notes, to-dos, habits, check-ins, name, and settings stay in this device's app storage. Pace does not use ads, advertising trackers, or analytics.

If you sign in, Pace uses Google Firebase for account sign-in and to save your calendar under your account. This includes your email address, account ID, name if you add one, and the planner content you choose to save. The app is designed to keep each signed-in account's data separate. Google processes this information to provide Firebase services. Pace does not sell your data.

When signed in, the same planner data is stored on your device and synced to your Firebase account. Internet access is needed for sign-in and syncing. Download a copy from Settings before removing data you may need.

Optional features

Calendar import: Pace reads the calendar file you choose on your device and copies its events into Pace. It does not connect to your old calendar service or change the file. Imported events follow the same storage rule: local when signed out, and synced to your account when signed in.

Speech-to-text: if you tap Speak, Pace asks your browser or device speech service to turn your words into text. That service may process audio under its own privacy policy. Pace saves the resulting words with your task and does not save a dictation recording. Typing is always available.

Audio notes: if you tap Start recording in a task, Pace records up to 45 seconds and saves that recording with the task. It stays on this device when you are signed out; when signed in, it is included in your synced calendar data. Pace does not record until you start it.

Photo reading: if you choose to scan handwriting, Pace reads the photo on your device using its bundled reading tool. Pace does not upload the photo.

Reminders: Pace uses local notifications on the device; they are not sent from a Pace server. Browser reminders may require Pace to remain open. Delivery depends on the operating system and the permissions you choose.

Children

Pace is not designed for children under 13, and children should not create accounts. If an adult chooses to add family information, it is handled like the adult's other planner content: stored on the device when signed out and synced to the adult's account when signed in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with a new date at the top.
