Where is my information?

When you are signed out, your planner stays in this device’s app storage. When you sign in, Pace also saves it under your account in Google Firebase so it can sync to another device. Deleting the app removes the local copy, but does not sign you out or remove the cloud copy. Use Settings → Delete my account if you want to remove your account data; download a copy first.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

Check that reminders are on in Pace Settings and allowed in your device settings. Browser reminders may need Pace to stay open. On iPhone, delivery depends on notification permission and iOS settings.

How does photo reading work?

The handwriting reader is built into Pace. Photos are read on your device and are not uploaded by Pace. Clear, straight-on photos work best.
